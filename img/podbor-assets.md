# Изображения страницы «Подбор дисков»

Обновлены 16 октября 2026 года. Все изображения страницы загружаются локально.

## Фотореалистичные сцены

Созданы встроенным инструментом Imagegen, без API/CLI. Исходные PNG сохранены
в стандартной папке генерации Codex, веб-версии — WebP шириной 1200 px.
Сцена телефона — визуализация сценария, не скриншот действующего интерфейса.

- `podbor-selection.webp` — автомобиль и семь дисков разного дизайна и диаметра.
- `podbor-tryon.webp` — человек наводит телефон на автомобиль; на экране выбранные диски.

### Полный промпт первого изображения

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: photographic hero image for Russian car wheel selection service, landscape 3:2. Create a convincingly real premium automotive editorial photograph, not illustration or CGI. A real metallic silver modern European sedan seen from front three-quarter angle in an uncluttered warm grey automotive showroom. On the ground next to and in front of the car stand seven separate alloy wheel rims WITHOUT tires, clearly different diameters and spoke designs: a small simple silver five-spoke rim, dark split spokes, thin silver multispokes, bronze six spokes, large black mesh, and other distinct sizes. Rims stand upright in a loose natural group, with correct perspective, realistic metallic texture, believable shadows and no deformation. The scene communicates uncertainty about which size and design will fit the car. Whole car and rims visible together; rims prominent enough to recognize at mobile size. Soft natural side light, quiet off-white and warm grey palette, subtle terracotta environment accents. No people, no text, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no collage, no graphic overlays. Main subjects centrally framed with safe margins, image will be displayed as a website card.
```

### Полный промпт второго изображения

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape 3:2 website feature photograph for virtual wheel try-on service. A realistic over-the-shoulder close photograph of an adult person in a neutral beige jacket holding a modern smartphone horizontally with both hands, aiming its camera at their parked metallic graphite sporty sedan in a bright clean outdoor parking area. The real car is visible in the middle distance with ordinary simple silver wheels. The phone screen is large, sharp and readable in the foreground and shows THE SAME car in the SAME angle and environment with upgraded thin silver multispoke alloy wheels, making the wheel try-on unmistakable. A tasteful camera/app interface on the screen has a small row of three different rim thumbnails at the bottom and one round capture button, minimalist warm white styling with tiny terracotta selection accent. No text inside the UI, no brand logos. This is a conceptual visualization of choosing and viewing rims using a car photo, not a magical hologram. Real human hands with anatomically correct fingers, realistic phone, glass reflections, natural daylight, gentle depth of field but both the car on screen and real car recognizable. Clean editorial commercial photography, not illustration, not CGI, no floating objects, no extra fingers, no watermark. Composition safe for a website image card, main action centrally framed.
```
